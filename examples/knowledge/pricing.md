# Nimbus Cloud Pricing (2026)

Nimbus Cloud is a fictional managed vector-database and inference platform used as Atlas's demo knowledge base.

## Plans

### Starter
- $0 per month, forever free.
- 1 project, 2 GB vector storage, 100,000 queries per month.
- Community support only. No SLA.

### Growth
- $249 per month, billed monthly, or $2,490 per year (two months free).
- 10 projects, 200 GB vector storage, 25 million queries per month.
- Email support with a 1 business-day first response target.
- 99.9% monthly uptime SLA.

### Enterprise
- Custom pricing, minimum annual commitment of $60,000.
- Unlimited projects, dedicated clusters, private networking (VPC peering) and SSO/SAML.
- 24/7 phone support with a 30-minute first response target for Severity 1 incidents.
- 99.99% monthly uptime SLA.

## Overage pricing

Usage beyond the plan allowance is billed at:
- Storage: $0.18 per GB-month.
- Queries: $0.40 per additional 100,000 queries.
- Inference (embedding generation): $0.02 per 1,000 documents embedded.

## Discounts

- Startups under 2 years old with less than $5M in funding receive 50% off Growth for 12 months (program code NIMBUS-LAUNCH).
- Registered non-profits and universities receive 30% off any paid plan.
- Annual prepayment on Growth is equivalent to a 16.7% discount versus monthly billing.
